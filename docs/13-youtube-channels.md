# 13 · Free YouTube Channels

> ✅ Every channel/video URL below was checked via direct fetch or corroborating search results in August 2026 — see the verification note at the bottom. All are free.

## How to use this list
Don't binge-watch all of these. Pick **one** foundations channel + **one** framework-specific channel, follow along by building, then come back for the rest as reference.

## Foundations (how LLMs/agents actually work)

| Channel | Why | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Andrej Karpathy** | Ex-OpenAI/Tesla AI lead. His "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" and "Let's build GPT" series are widely considered the best free deep-dive into how transformers/LLMs actually work under the hood — build one from scratch on screen. | [youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy](https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy) |
| **3Blue1Brown** | Not agent-specific, but its neural network / transformer / attention visual explainers give you the math intuition every other resource assumes you already have. | [youtube.com/@3blue1brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown) |
| **IBM Technology** | Short (5–15 min), clear conceptual explainers on RAG, agents, MCP, vector DBs — good for quickly filling a specific gap. | [youtube.com/@IBMTechnology/videos](https://www.youtube.com/@IBMTechnology/videos) |

## Official framework/vendor channels

| Channel | Why | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **LangChain (official)** | Produced by the core maintainers — idiomatic LangGraph/LangChain patterns straight from the source, avoids the "copying outdated blog code" trap. | [youtube.com/@LangChain](https://www.youtube.com/@LangChain) |
| **DeepLearning.AI (official)** | Andrew Ng's org — hosts the full free short courses (see [Ch. 12](12-courses-communities.md)) as YouTube videos too: RAG, agents, evals, fine-tuning. | [youtube.com/@Deeplearningai](https://www.youtube.com/@Deeplearningai) |
| **Anthropic (official)** | Product walkthroughs, engineering talks, and conference sessions (e.g. their AI Engineer summit talks) directly from the team that writes the docs cited throughout this repo. | [youtube.com/@anthropic-ai](https://www.youtube.com/@anthropic-ai) |

## Hands-on / build-along agent tutorials

| Channel | Why | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Sam Witteveen** | Google Developer Expert (ML), building with LLMs/agents since 2023. Dense, practical, code-first walkthroughs of LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, and raw agent loops — one of the most technically rigorous independent teachers in this space. | [youtube.com/@samwitteveenai](https://www.youtube.com/@samwitteveenai) |
| **freeCodeCamp.org** | Free, full-length (2–6 hour) project-based courses, several specifically on agents — e.g. *"AI Agents For Beginners"* (LLM fundamentals → multi-agent systems, ft. CodeCloud's Mumshad Mannambeth) and *"Agentic AI using LangGraph."* Best if you want a single long sitting rather than scattered videos. | [youtube.com/@freecodecamp](https://www.youtube.com/@freecodecamp) |
